How this is calculated

Savings is the opening balance. Income hits once on its paid-on date. Hard and soft items repeat from the next date. Category sub-rows hit once on their date. Monthly, quarterly and yearly keep the same calendar day, clamped to month length.

Hard/soft annualised rates use the amounts actually hitting in this horizon (including overrides), spread across the months. Extra income is a weekly top-up from today so the trough stays at or above the floor. An override changes that date only — the template amount on the left stays as the usual cycle.
